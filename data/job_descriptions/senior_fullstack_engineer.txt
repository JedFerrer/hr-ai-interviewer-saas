Senior Full-Stack Engineer

Company: TechCorp Inc.
Location: Remote (US)
Salary: $150,000 - $180,000

- Collaborate with product managers to translate requirements into technical solutions

Requirements:
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript/JavaScript and Python
- Experience with React (or similar frontend frameworks) and Node.js
- Solid understanding of relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience with cloud services (AWS or GCP)
- Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Strong communication skills and ability to work in a cross-functional team

Nice to Have:

- Previous experience in a startup or high-growth environment
